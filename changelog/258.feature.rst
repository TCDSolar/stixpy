Add background subtraction to `~stixpy.product.sources.science.ScienceData.get_data` with ``bkg=``, following STIX-GSW's ``stx_convert_pixel_data``. The background, pixel data with a single time bin such as a STIX background (BKG) file, is livetime and ELUT corrected, scaled to the duration of each selected time bin and subtracted, and the uncertainties of both products and of the livetime are combined. Spectrogram (``sci-xray-spec``) files can be background subtracted with a pixel-data background file, as in ``stx_convert_spectrogram``. A background whose energy bins, detectors or pixels do not match the science data raises an error.
