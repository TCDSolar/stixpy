`~stixpy.calibration.grid.get_grid_transmission` now takes the photon energies (as a `~astropy.units.Quantity`) and detectors as well as the flare location, ``get_grid_transmission(ph_energy, detectors, flare_location)``, and returns the energy-dependent grid transmission with shape ``(n_energies, n_detectors)``, corrected for internal shadowing and including the finest grids, as in STIX-GSW. The flare location can be a `~astropy.coordinates.SkyCoord` in the `~stixpy.coordinates.frames.STIXImaging` frame or a `~astropy.units.Quantity` ``[Tx, Ty]``. ``_calculate_grid_transmission`` has been removed.
