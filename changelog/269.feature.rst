Add `~stixpy.coordinates.fov.get_illumination_status` to check whether a source at a given location fully illuminates the detectors (``"full"``), shadows one pixel row (``"single row"``), also partially shadows the outer pixel columns (``"partial"``, the maximal field of view of the paper), or lies outside the field of view, following the geometry in Appendix C of Massa et al. (2023). It also returns which pixels to use (``"top+bot"``, ``"top"`` or ``"bot"``), which can be passed to `~stixpy.calibration.visibility.create_meta_pixels`. Locations in other frames, such as Helioprojective, are transformed to the STIX imaging frame at their ``obstime``. Add `~stixpy.visualisation.plotters.plot_fov` to plot the full sensitivity, single row and partial field of view boxes, optionally marking source locations with their status. The plot is drawn on a blank map in the STIX imaging frame by default, and source locations given in another frame are transformed to it. With ``frame`` the boxes and sources are plotted in that frame instead, for example Helioprojective with Solar Orbiter as observer, and ``draw_limb`` and ``draw_grid`` add the solar limb and heliographic grid.
