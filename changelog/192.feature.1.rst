`~stixpy.product.sources.science.ScienceData.get_data` can now return spectra ready for fitting with sunkit-spex (``sunkit_spex_spectrum=True``): one time range gives a ``sunkit_spex.spectrum.Spectrum``, several give an `~ndcube.NDCubeSequence` with one spectrum per range, and ``sunkit_spex_detector_sum=False`` gives an `~ndcube.NDCollection` with one spectrum per detector. Each spectrum holds its exposure time, spectral response matrix (SRM), photon energy edges, geometric area and Sun-spacecraft distance in its metadata. The SRM comes from the new `~stixpy.product.sources.science.ScienceData.get_masked_srm` and follows STIX-GSW's ``stx_convert_spectrogram2ospex``, including the grid transmission for a given ``flare_location``, the attenuator for each RCR state and hole tailing. ``sunkit_spex_systematic_error`` adds the standard STIX systematic uncertainty and ``srm_e_min`` sets the lowest photon energy of the SRM.
