Add more ways to select data in `~stixpy.product.sources.science.ScienceData.get_data`: energy ranges in keV (for example ``[[4, 10], [15, 28]] * u.keV``, taking the bins whose centres lie in each range), time ranges as strings or `~astropy.time.Time` (checked against the time range of the file), and the detector labels ``"top24"`` (the 24 imaging detectors, as in STIX-GSW) and ``"bkg"`` (the background detector with its small-aperture pixels 2 and 5).
