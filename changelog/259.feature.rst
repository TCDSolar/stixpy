`~stixpy.product.sources.science.ScienceData.get_data` can now return spectra ready for fitting with sunkit-spex (``sunkit_spex_spectrum=True``), following STIX-GSW's ``stx_convert_pixel_data`` and ``stx_convert_spectrogram2ospex``: one time range gives a ``sunkit_spex.spectrum.Spectrum``, several give an `~ndcube.NDCubeSequence` with one spectrum per range, and ``sunkit_spex_detector_sum=False`` gives an `~ndcube.NDCollection` with one spectrum (or sequence) per detector or detector range. Each spectrum holds its counts, uncertainties, live time as exposure time, spectral response matrix from `~stixpy.calibration.srm.get_srm` for its RCR state, photon energy edges, geometric area, flare angle and Sun-spacecraft distance. ``flare_location``, a `~astropy.coordinates.SkyCoord` in the STIX imaging or any other frame, sets the grid transmission and the flare angle; ``sunkit_spex_systematic_error`` adds the standard STIX systematic uncertainty (7% below 7 keV, 5% to 10 keV, 3% above) and ``srm_e_min`` sets the lowest photon energy of the response. This needs the new optional ``spectroscopy`` extra, ``pip install 'stixpy[spectroscopy]'``.
