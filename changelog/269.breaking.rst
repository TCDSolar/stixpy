The `~stixpy.coordinates.frames.STIXImaging` frame now has a single ``obstime`` and the ``obstime_end`` attribute and ``obstime_avg`` property have been removed (see `#217 <https://github.com/TCDSolar/stixpy/issues/217>`__). For an observation over a time range pass its average time as ``obstime``, for example ``STIXImaging(obstime=time_range.center)`` instead of ``STIXImaging(obstime=time_range.start, obstime_end=time_range.end)``; the time range itself stays with the data, such as the ``DATE-BEG`` and ``DATE-END`` of a map. STIX maps now give their frame the ``DATE-AVG`` of the header (or ``DATE-OBS`` if it is missing) rather than ``DATE-BEG``, and the transforms to and from Helioprojective use the spacecraft pointing and position at that time instead of their average over the time range, which changes results slightly. As a result, off-disk coordinates now transform between the STIX imaging and Helioprojective frames without becoming NaN.
