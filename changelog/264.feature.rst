`~stixpy.product.sources.science.ScienceData.get_data` has a new ``error_model`` keyword. The default, ``"legacy"``, combines the uncertainties as before. ``"correlated"`` keeps the statistical, livetime and background uncertainties apart until every sum is done, and adds each as its correlations need: the livetime uncertainty linearly over the pixels and energies of a detector and over the two detectors on one trigger counter, and the background uncertainty linearly over time bins, as one background measurement is scaled to all of them. It also gives a time range the summed duration of its bins, leaving out gaps and keeps negative background-subtracted counts. For both models, ``sum_all_times`` now adds the livetime uncertainty in quadrature rather than as an RMS, and with ``bkg`` the livetime fraction no longer has an energy axis, which fixes the exposure time of per-detector ``sunkit_spex`` spectrum sequences, which was summed over the energy bins.
