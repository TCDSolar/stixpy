Add livetime, energy look-up table (ELUT) and background corrections to `~stixpy.product.sources.science.ScienceData.get_data`, following STIX-GSW's ``stx_convert_pixel_data``. A background product given with ``bkg=`` is scaled to the duration of each selected time bin and subtracted, and the uncertainties of both products and of the livetime are combined. Spectrogram (``sci-xray-spec``) files can be background subtracted with a pixel-data background file, as in ``stx_convert_spectrogram``.
